After a period of time, [MaaS
models](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/models/partner-models/use-partner-models) are
deprecated and typically replaced with newer model versions. This page lists
deprecated MaaS models and their shutdown dates to help you plan and migrate to
newer model versions.

## Anthropic's Claude 3 Haiku on Google Cloud

Anthropic's Claude 3 Haiku on Google Cloud is **deprecated as of February 23, 2026** and will be
**shut down on August 23, 2026**. Anthropic's Claude 3 Haiku on Google Cloud is available to
existing customers only.

Anthropic's Claude 3 Haiku on Google Cloud is Anthropic's fastest vision and text model for
near-instant responses to basic queries, meant for seamless AI experiences
mimicking human interactions.

and market trends, and forecasting.
